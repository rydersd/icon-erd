# ICONERD backlog

Owner-requested additions, 2026-10-02. Larger artboards and gradients remain planned work. Library output profiles now provide menu bar template assets, Mac app raster catalogs and PC app PNG sizes; see [library output](library-output.md). Existing app-icon mode provides a 24-unit vector canvas, colored background/artwork layers and 1024px export; it does not yet provide the complete workflow below.

| ID | Work | Acceptance outline | Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|
| APP-01 | macOS and Windows app-icon workflow | Dedicated editing/review targets for Mac and PC apps; background options, safe-area/mask previews and platform/size presets. Export individual raster/vector sizes, with ICNS/ICO or platform asset packaging planned explicitly. Preserve editable originals and provenance. | Existing app-icon template/export is the starting point; establish target-specific size, alpha and packaging requirements before implementation. |
| APP-02 | Larger editable artboards and scalable authoring | Configurable width/height and authoring-unit presets beyond the existing 24-unit plane (for example 64/128/256/1024 units); align grid, rulers, guides, snap, radii, stroke weights, previews and export. Distinguish resizing the artboard from scaling its artwork. | Versioned canvas metrics and backward-compatible 24-unit imports; audit hardcoded canvas constants and component transform/tolerance units. |
| APP-03 | Menu-bar and small-system-icon targets | Dedicated menu-bar icon presets and actual-size previews, light/dark contexts, monochrome/template artwork options and intentional small-size variants. Keep detail legible when derived from larger app artwork; permit separate optical edits instead of only automatic scaling. | Target-specific appearance/export rules, source-vs-variant relationship and multi-size review. |
| PAINT-01 | Solid and gradient paint model | Keep existing solid colors; add editable linear/radial gradient fills and strokes, ordered stops, color/opacity, direction/position and object-vs-canvas coordinates. On-canvas handles, theme semantics, Undo/Redo, component propagation and JSON/ZIP persistence agree with rendering. | Versioned paint schema, explicit instance-local vs definition-shared paint policy and export validation. |
| PAINT-02 | Gradient meshes | Editable mesh topology, control positions and per-node color/opacity; direct selection, consistent interpolation, Undo and persistence. Render/export must retain appearance or provide an explicit fallback. | Gradient paint foundation; choose patch/topology/interpolation model, color space and evaluator. Verify renderer/export interoperability before selecting a vector mesh format; plan raster fallback and native IllTool mapping. |

Review APP-01 and PAINT-01 before PAINT-02. Gradient meshes require a geometry/color evaluator and serialization design; a gradient-looking preview alone is not support. The private wiki tracks native ownership proposals and acceptance examples. Direct Figma publishing, LLM usage-based organization, nested component definitions and remote library sync remain proposals and require their own scopes.
